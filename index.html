Method

Four beats, no detours.

1

Fund

Dollar-pegged coins turn into a base amount, quietly doing their job on Robinhood Chain.

2

Grow

Returns accumulate with each block, tracked apart from the base amount so growth and base cannot blur.

3

Tap

Checkout consumes growth alone. A purchase bigger than that sum is refused, flat out.

4

Exit

Move the base amount out to an address you control, at a moment of your choosing. No other event can set it off.

Ground rules

Small surface. Hard edges.

Dual counters

The base amount and grown portion are tracked apart. Purchases can reach just the latter.

Refusal over compromise

When a purchase exceeds what has grown, the purchase is turned away entirely. No gap is covered from the base.

Silence means stop

When a chain lookup stalls or a figure is absent, the usable amount counts as empty, so the purchase is turned away.

A lone venue

Growth originates in a lone venue on Robinhood Chain: ordinary borrower fees, with no gearing and no exotic tactics.

Exit on command

The base amount departs solely at your instruction, fully, or by portions, sent to an address you control.

Moves with demand

Returns drift with borrowing demand. Each figure on this page is a reading, not a promise.
